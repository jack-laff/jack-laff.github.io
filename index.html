About

I started out on building sites and in a garage, which is where I learned to build things and fix them when they break. That led me to mechanical engineering at Imperial and to a year in industry at Adia Thermal, a 10-person heat pump controls startup, where I owned all of the hardware: design, testing, suppliers and production.

I'm now focused on robotics and automation. My final year project develops a camera module with machine learning defect detection for a robotic battery cell assembly line, and I'm building a vision-guided pick-and-place arm in ROS 2 alongside a robotic manipulators project.
