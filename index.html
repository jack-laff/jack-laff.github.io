About

I started out on building sites and in a garage, which is where I learned to build things and fix them when they break. That led me to mechanical engineering at Imperial and to a year in industry at Adia Thermal, a 10-person heat pump controls startup, where I owned the hardware: design, testing, suppliers and production.

Before that I was suspension designer and project manager for Imperial's Formula Student team, contributing to a 10.1% vehicle mass reduction. Outside work I've built a DC motor from scratch, restored an outboard boat engine and repaired a motorcycle.
