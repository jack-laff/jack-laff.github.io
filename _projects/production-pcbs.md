## The problem

Some production steps relied on hand-soldered wiring. These steps were slow, fiddly and easy to get wrong.

## What I did

I designed custom PCBs in KiCad, made by PCBWay, to remove or simplify those steps. In the example below, a single
flexible PCB replaces a complicated hand-soldered wire assembly inside one of the devices.
